Partial Routing Data

Each relay only sees the previous and next hops—never the whole path or both sender/recipient.

Layered Encryption

Traffic is encrypted at each hop (onion routing). No relay can decrypt the full payload.

Periodic Circuit Rotation

Paths auto-rotate, making long-term tracking and correlation much harder.

Metadata Reduction

Timing/route info split up, reducing scope for adversaries and fingerprinters.

Security Model

Privacy Through Distribution

BlackNet makes it extremely difficult for any one relay operator, ISP, or even nation-state to deanonymize users by splitting all knowledge/metadata between nodes.

  • No single observer can correlate sender and receiver
  • Layered encryption with a separate key for every relay
  • No centralized logging or authorities
  • Circuits and ISPs are isolated
⚠ Disclaimer: No system can guarantee absolute anonymity.
BlackNet raises the bar, but your threat model and endpoint security are crucial.
